## Overview

Ahoge is a plugin for Autodesk Maya designed to simplify and enhance the process of creating digital hair. Our goal is to make creating digital hair as intuitive and effortless as possible.

Ahoge ships as a Maya module: the `ahogeShape` node, the Nurbopus and Swirl nodes, the Ahoge tools window, the outliner and the brush tools, rendering through [Arnold, 3Delight or RenderMan](/documentation/renderers).
