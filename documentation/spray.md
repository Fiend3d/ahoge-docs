# Spray

**Spray** scatters hair roots around the placement area instead of keeping them locked to the regular grid on the surface.

- Use spray to break the perfect grid look of generated hair, especially for short, spiky or flyaway strands.
- Spray works together with [Random Placement](/documentation/noise#random-placement): random placement scrambles the placement positions, spray offsets the roots from the surface.
- Spray amount is authored with a ramp, so you can spray only the tips region or only an area of the head.

## Tips

- Small spray values plus [Clump](/documentation/clump) give a natural, slightly untidy look.
- Large spray values with low [density](/documentation/segments-and-density) are useful for stray hairs around the hairline.
- Spray is part of the attribute groups copied by **Paste Attributes** — see [Misc tools](/documentation/misc).

The full Spray rollout, with attribute names, defaults and what each one does, is on the [ahogeShape attributes](/documentation/nodes/ahoge-shape#spray) page.
