# Clump

**Clump** pulls strands together into groups, so hair reads as locks instead of individually scattered curves.

- Low clump keeps hair airy and loose.
- High clump produces distinct locks and clean silhouette edges.
- Clump is authored with a ramp, so you can clump the roots and keep the tips loose (or the other way around).

## Combining clump with other attributes

| Look | Combination |
|---|---|
| Neat, combed hair | [Density](/documentation/segments-and-density) medium + clump high + low [noise](/documentation/noise) |
| Windblown hair | clump medium + noise high + [spray](/documentation/spray) low |
| Wet hair | clump high + [width](/documentation/width) low + multistrand low |
| Fuzzy hairline | clump low + spray high |

Clump is one of the attribute groups transferred by **Paste Attributes** — see [Misc tools](/documentation/misc).

See [Clump attributes](/documentation/nodes/ahoge-shape#clump) for individual controls and defaults.
