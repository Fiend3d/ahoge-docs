## About the project

Ahoge is made by a group of artists with two decades of experience in full-length animated film. The project exists to share that experience and to build a community around creating digital hair.

Artists featured in the demo section:

- **Alina Pavlova** — Penelope / Poppy, the project mascot — [pavlalinka on ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/pavlalinka)
- **Sergey Ostankov** — Harry Potter — [fbios on ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/fbios)

## About this site

This site is a reorganization of the Ahoge documentation, written from the official wiki at [ahoge.wiki.gd](https://ahoge.wiki.gd/). It is not a substitute for the official wiki — where the two differ, the official wiki and the current build of Ahoge are authoritative.

Ahoge is a commercial project: downloads, news and updates are published on [Boosty](https://boosty.to/ahoge).
